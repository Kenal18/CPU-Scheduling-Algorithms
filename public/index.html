      Every decision the scheduler made, in order. Select a line to jump the timeline to that moment.

          Ties are broken by arrival time, then by process number. In Round Robin, a process arriving at the same moment a quantum ends queues ahead of the one that was interrupted. A context switch is charged whenever the CPU moves from one process to a different one.
